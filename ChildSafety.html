Child Safety & Protection Standards

MaPa Care — Published standards to prevent and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE/CSAM).

1. Our Commitment

MaPa Care strictly prohibits any content, activity, or behaviour that promotes, depicts, or facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE/CSAM). We are committed to protecting minors and vulnerable individuals and complying with all applicable laws, regulations, and platform safety standards.

2. Age Requirements & Private Family Pairing

  • All users must be at least 18 years old to register for and use MaPa Care.
  • MaPa Care is a private family-paired elder-care application; it does not provide public chat rooms or open social discovery. Accounts found to be operated by minors or violating age requirements are suspended or deleted.

3. Prevention & Detection

  • Automated systems: Our AI and content moderation tools block and flag potentially exploitative, abusive, or unsafe content.
  • Manual review: Our safety team investigates flagged reports and support tickets and escalates confirmed violations immediately.
  • Proactive audits: We periodically review platform safety controls to prevent misuse of voice, media, or messaging features.

4. Reporting Mechanisms (In-App & Direct)

Users can report safety concerns or abuse directly within the MaPa Care app via the Support / Help screen or by contacting our safety team via email. All reports related to child safety or abuse are prioritized for immediate review and action.

If anyone is in immediate danger: Contact your local emergency services first, then report the incident to us for internal review and escalation.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

We fully comply with applicable child protection and cybercrime laws. Any verified CSAE/CSAM incidents are promptly reported to relevant law enforcement authorities and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable. We preserve necessary records and metadata for law enforcement when legally required.

6. Enforcement

  • Accounts found in violation of our child protection standards will be immediately and permanently banned.
  • All violating content is removed immediately, and relevant evidence is preserved securely for law enforcement investigation.

7. Training & Internal Policies

Our staff and moderators follow strict internal escalation protocols and best practices for handling safety-related reports and maintaining compliance with Google Play and regulatory child safety standards.
