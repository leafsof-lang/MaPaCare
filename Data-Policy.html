MaPa Care — Data Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MaPa Care, developed and managed by Leafsof Technologies. This Data Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how you can control or delete your data.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use MaPa Care:

  • Personal & Account information: name, phone number, email address, account role (Elder or Family), language preference, and family pairing records.
  • Care & Wellness records: medicine schedules, medicine adherence logs, daily check-ins, mood selections, doctor appointments, care reminders, and emergency contacts.
  • Voice & Companion content: voice interactions with the AI companion, daily summary digests, family voice reminder recordings, and uploaded family moments.
  • Location data: device location only when location sharing is turned on for the paired elder profile. When enabled, a visible banner is displayed on the elder's screen.
  • Usage & Technical data: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, push notification tokens, and diagnostic logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI companion conversations, voice medicine reminders, daily check-ins, and SOS emergency calling.
  • To share plain-language daily digests, medicine statuses, and safety alerts with the paired family caregiver (while keeping exact conversation words private to the elder).
  • To improve speech recognition, reliability, and user experience across supported languages.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, or security threats and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal or health data. We may share data only with:

  • Your paired Family / Elder account as consented during pairing setup.
  • Service providers who help operate our infrastructure (e.g., cloud hosting, AI/speech processing, push notifications) under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal authorities when required by applicable law.

5. Security

All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. We apply strict access controls and secure storage practices. We never ask elders for money, bank details, or OTPs.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • We retain personal and care data while your account is active.
  • Voice reminder templates and check-in history can be managed or removed within the app.
  • If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data and paired elder/family records within 30 days, unless required by law to retain specific records.
  • Anonymized or aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely.
How to delete your account & data
  1. Open the MaPa Care app → Settings / Support → request account deletion, OR
  2. Send an email to leafsof@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MaPa Care account" and include your registered email address or phone number.

We will verify your request and permanently delete your account, profile, medicine logs, check-in history, and voice recordings within 30 days.

7. User Controls

  • You can edit or delete medicines, reminders, emergency contacts, and voice recordings within the app.
  • You can enable or disable location sharing at any time.
  • To request a copy or deletion of your data, contact leafsof@gmail.com.

8. Children

This app is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Data Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
