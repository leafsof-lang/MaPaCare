MaPa Care — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MaPa Care (“we”, “our”, “us”), developed and operated by Leafsof Technologies, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the MaPa Care elder-care and family companion application and related services. By using the app, you agree to the terms outlined below.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: name, phone number, email address, role (Elder or Family member), preferred language (e.g., English, Hindi, Tamil), and pairing details linking an elder profile with their authorized family caregiver.
  • Care, Wellness & Medicine Data: medicine names, schedules, adherence logs (taken/missed/upcoming), daily check-ins, mood selections, appointments, care reminders, and emergency contact numbers provided by you or your family caregiver.
  • Voice & AI Companion Data: audio input and transcripts when using the Talk / AI Companion feature, as well as family voice message recordings (such as medicine or hydration reminders recorded in a family member's voice) and shared family moments.
  • Location Data: approximate or precise device location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) only when location sharing is explicitly enabled between the paired elder and family account. The elder is always shown a clear indicator when location sharing is active.
  • Device Permissions & Technical Data: microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO) for voice interaction, phone call permission (CALL_PHONE) to dial emergency contacts when the SOS feature is triggered, notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) for medicine and check-in alerts, device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and diagnostic logs.

3. Elder Privacy & Family Sharing Boundaries

MaPa Care is designed around mutual trust and explicit consent between elders and their family caregivers:

  • What the paired family caregiver can see: daily check-in status, mood, medicine adherence (taken, missed, or upcoming), plain-language daily summaries/digests, safety alerts, and location (only while location sharing is turned on).
  • What stays private to the elder: the exact word-for-word transcripts of the elder's conversations with the AI companion, and anything the elder explicitly asks the companion not to share.
  • Informed consent: elders are shown what information is shared with their family caregiver during setup before using the app.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, pair, and manage Elder and Family accounts.
  • To deliver medicine reminders, daily check-ins, voice reminders, and AI companion conversations in the user's preferred language.
  • To generate plain-language daily digests and safety alerts for paired family caregivers.
  • To facilitate emergency SOS calls and optional real-time location sharing.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud or misuse, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Processing

Temporary verification codes (such as OTPs or pairing codes) and live speech recognition streams are processed ephemerally where applicable. Profile records, medicine schedules, check-in history, and daily digests are stored securely on our servers so the app can function across paired devices until deleted.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal or health-related data. We never ask elders for money, bank details, or OTPs. We share information only:

  • Between paired accounts: sharing check-ins, medicine adherence, digests, alerts, and consented location with your linked family caregiver.
  • With trusted service providers: infrastructure, hosting, speech/AI processing, and push notification providers operating under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal or safety reasons: when required by law, legal process, or to protect the vital safety of our users.

7. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained while your account remains active.
  • If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.
  • Anonymized or aggregated technical data may be retained for service reliability and analytics.

8. Your Rights & Controls

  • You can update or delete profile details, medicines, reminders, and emergency contacts directly in the app.
  • Location sharing can be turned off at any time through the app settings.
  • You can request a copy of your data or full account deletion through the app or by emailing leafsof@gmail.com.

9. Children

MaPa Care is intended for adult family caregivers and senior citizens (users aged 18 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a minor has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

All communication between the MaPa Care app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal, voice, and wellness information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective date." Continued use of MaPa Care after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
